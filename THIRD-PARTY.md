## ReShade 6.8.0

- What: `ReShade64.asi` in `reshade-6.8.0-addon.zip` is `ReShade64.dll` from the official add-on installer `ReShade_Setup_6.8.0_Addon.exe` (https://reshade.me, sha256 `afe4c8f1...6445`), unchanged and renamed so the ASI loader picks it up; its license is in the zip as `reshade-shaders/LICENSE-ReShade.md`. The shader headers `ReShade.fxh` and `ReShadeUI.fxh` come from https://github.com/crosire/reshade-shaders at `fd002217` (CC0-1.0).
